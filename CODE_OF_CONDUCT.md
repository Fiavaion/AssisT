# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

AssisT exists to make the web more accessible to neurodivergent learners. Contributing to a project with that purpose requires us to treat every contributor — new or experienced, disabled or not — with the same care we ask the web to extend to our users.

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language.
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints, approaches, and experiences.
- Gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and for the neurodivergent learners AssisT serves.
- Showing empathy toward other community members.
- Assuming good faith, especially when communicating across languages, cultures, and cognitive styles.
- Describing technical decisions clearly and without jargon where possible; accessibility of discourse matters as much as accessibility of code.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment of any kind.
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or electronic address, without explicit permission.
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.
- Deliberately dismissing or mocking requests framed around accessibility, neurodiversity, or disability.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces — the code repository, issue trackers, pull requests, discussions, project email addresses, and any real-world events associated with the project. It also applies when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported confidentially by contacting `conduct@fiavaion.com`. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated and will result in a response that is appropriate to the circumstances.

The project maintainer is responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and is expected to take fair and appropriate corrective action in response to any instance of unacceptable behaviour.

Project maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers will follow these guidelines in determining the consequences of actions they deem in violation of this Code of Conduct.

1. **Correction** — Private written warning from maintainers, providing clarity about the nature of the violation and why the behaviour was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.
2. **Warning** — A warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with involved parties (including unsolicited interaction with maintainers) for a specified period. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.
3. **Temporary ban** — A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved is allowed during this period.
4. **Permanent ban** — A permanent ban from any sort of public interaction with the community.
