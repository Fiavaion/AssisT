## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select a passage of dense academic text and click Simplify in the Quick Actions toolbar.

AssisT rewrites the text at a lower reading level — breaking up long sentences, replacing jargon with plain alternatives, and restructuring passive constructions.

This is semantic rewriting, not just vocabulary substitution. The AI understands the meaning of what it's simplifying, which is why it handles discipline-specific academic language far better than rule-based tools.

The simplified version appears alongside the original so you can compare. You can read the simplified version aloud, copy it, or use it as a starting point for your own notes.

This is particularly valuable for students with reading comprehension differences, those reading in a second language, or anyone working with content significantly above their current reading level."
