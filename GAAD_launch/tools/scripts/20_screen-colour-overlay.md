## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the popup and toggle Screen Colour Overlay on.

A preset tint is applied immediately. You can choose from a range of colours — yellow, cream, blue, green, pink, and custom — using a colour picker.

The opacity is adjustable from barely visible to a strong tint, depending on what's comfortable for you.

This is designed for students with Meares-Irlen syndrome, also known as visual stress, where white backgrounds and high-contrast text cause distortion, headaches, or eye strain. A light-coloured overlay reduces the perceived contrast of the page.

It's also useful for anyone who finds bright screens uncomfortable for extended reading — which covers a wide range of students, not just those with a formal diagnosis.

The overlay applies globally across all browser tabs while it's active."
