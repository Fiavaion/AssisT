## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select the full text of an assignment brief and click 'Break Down' from the Quick Actions toolbar.

AssisT analyses the brief and returns a numbered checklist: research steps, drafting stages, formatting requirements, submission instructions — each as a discrete, actionable task.

For students with ADHD or executive function difficulties, the hardest part of an assignment is often knowing where to start. A clear task list removes that barrier.

The checklist appears in a panel you can copy or print. Each item is specific enough to be actionable — not generic advice like 'do research', but 'find three peer-reviewed sources on X'.

You can also ask AssisT to estimate time for each step — giving you a rough schedule you can work backwards from the submission deadline."
