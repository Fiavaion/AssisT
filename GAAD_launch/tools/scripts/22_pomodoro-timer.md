## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the popup and launch the Pomodoro Timer. A small floating widget appears in the corner of your screen.

By default it's set to twenty-five minutes of work followed by a five-minute break — the classic Pomodoro technique.

You can customise both durations in settings to match your needs. Some students with ADHD do better with shorter cycles — ten and three minutes, for example.

Start the timer, work through your material, and when the timer completes AssisT plays a gentle notification sound and switches automatically to the break timer.

The widget is draggable, so you can position it wherever it's least intrusive on your screen.

Structured time blocks with enforced breaks are one of the most evidence-supported strategies for managing attention difficulties during study."
