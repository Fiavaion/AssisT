**Category: Reading Aids** | Upload order: 13

## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Reading Progress shows you a visual progress bar at the top of the page so you always know how far through a document you are."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Enable Reading Progress from the popup.

A thin coloured bar appears at the top of the browser window. As you scroll, it fills from left to right, showing what percentage of the page you've read.

This is a small but meaningful feature for students who find long pages daunting. Knowing you're sixty percent through a reading — rather than having no sense of how long it is — reduces anxiety and helps with planning.

It also helps when returning to a page: a quick glance tells you whether you've already worked through most of the material.

The bar colour matches your AssisT accent colour and can be adjusted in settings."
