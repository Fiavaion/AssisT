## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select a passage of text on any webpage — a dense paragraph, a long article, an assignment brief — and click Summarise in the Quick Actions toolbar.

AssisT sends the text to your chosen AI mode. The summary appears in a panel on the right within seconds.

Here's what makes AssisT's AI different: you have three privacy-respecting options.

With Local AI, AssisT uses Ollama running on your own machine — no internet required, no data sent anywhere. Models like Qwen3 and Llama run entirely on your CPU or GPU.

With Browser AI, the model runs directly in your browser using WebGPU. No server, no account. The model downloads once and runs locally from that point on.

With Cloud AI, AssisT uses Gemini or Claude for more powerful results — appropriate when privacy of the specific content is not a concern.

All three modes are available in the AssisT settings panel. The default is off — you enable only what you want."
