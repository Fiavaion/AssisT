## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"On any webpage — a journal article, a news piece, a blog post — click Citation Analyser from the AssisT popup.

AssisT evaluates the source across several dimensions: publication type and credibility, author credentials if identifiable, date and currency, potential ideological or commercial bias, and the strength of the evidence presented.

It returns a plain-language assessment: 'This is a peer-reviewed article published in a reputable journal. The methodology is sound. Note that the author has a funding relationship with the pharmaceutical company discussed.'

This supports research literacy — a skill that many students, particularly those new to academic research, find genuinely difficult.

The key claims are also extracted separately, making it easy to see what the source actually argues before deciding whether to include it in your work."
