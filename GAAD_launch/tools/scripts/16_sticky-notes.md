# Sticky Notes

**Category: Reading Aids** | Upload order: 16

---

## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Sticky Notes attaches colour-coded notes to any webpage — and they're still there the next time you visit."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the popup and click 'New Sticky Note', or use the keyboard shortcut.

A draggable sticky note appears on the page. Type your note, pick a colour, and position it wherever you want.

When you navigate away and return to that URL, the note is exactly where you left it.

This is great for leaving yourself reminders on any page you regularly visit — research sources, reading lists, article pages — or for annotating content with personal context.

You can have multiple sticky notes on a single page. Each one is independently positioned, coloured, and closeable.

Notes are stored by URL, so they follow the page rather than the browser tab."
