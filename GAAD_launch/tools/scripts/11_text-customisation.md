**Category: Reading Aids** | Upload order: 11

---

## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Text Customisation gives you full control over fonts, sizes, and spacing — meeting WCAG 2.2 Success Criterion 1.4.12 out of the box."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the AssisT popup and go to the Reading Aids tab, then Text Customisation.

You can adjust font family — including dyslexia-friendly options like OpenDyslexic and Atkinson Hyperlegible.

Font size scales independently of the browser's zoom level, so you can increase text size without disrupting a page's layout.

Line height, letter spacing, and word spacing each have their own slider. These four properties are exactly what WCAG Success Criterion 1.4.12 requires content to support — AssisT provides them for every page, even those that don't natively support user overrides.

Changes apply live as you drag the sliders. Your settings persist across sessions and across every page you visit."
