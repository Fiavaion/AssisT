# Reading Mode

**Category: Reading Help** | Upload order: 4

---

## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Reading Mode strips any webpage down to just its content — no navigation, no sidebars, no clutter."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open any busy webpage — a news article, a research site, a blog post — and click the AssisT icon, then toggle Reading Mode on.

The page reflows immediately. Navigation bars, sidebars, banners, ads, and decorative elements disappear, leaving only the article content.

AssisT uses Mozilla Readability — the same engine that powers Firefox Reader View — to identify and extract the main content. It works on any page, not just learning platforms.

Once in Reading Mode, all AssisT features still work: Read Aloud, Dictionary, Annotations. You're not losing any functionality.

To exit, toggle Reading Mode off in the popup — the page restores instantly.

This is especially helpful for students with ADHD or cognitive processing differences who find busy page layouts difficult to work with."
