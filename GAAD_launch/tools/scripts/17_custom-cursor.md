# Custom Cursor

**Category: Pointer & Zoom** | Upload order: 17

---

## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"The Custom Cursor replaces Chrome's default pointer with large, high-contrast styles — making it much easier to find and track on screen."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the popup, go to Pointer and Zoom, and select a cursor style.

AssisT offers several options: a large black cursor, a large white cursor with black outline, an enlarged default cursor, and a pointer-specific style for links.

The cursor size scales from one-and-a-half to three times the default size.

This is most useful for students with low vision, motor control differences who benefit from a larger target, or anyone who regularly loses the cursor on a busy page.

The custom cursor applies across every page while AssisT is active."
