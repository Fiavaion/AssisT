**Category: Pointer & Zoom** | Upload order: 18

---

## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"The Magnifying Lens is a circular zoom window that follows your cursor — magnifying content at up to four times without changing your browser zoom level."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Toggle the Magnifying Lens on from the popup.

A circular lens immediately appears around your cursor, magnifying whatever is underneath it at a zoom level you set — from one-and-a-half to four times.

Move your cursor across the page and the lens moves with it. Text, images, and diagrams all magnify cleanly.

Unlike browser zoom, the Magnifying Lens doesn't reflow the page or break the layout. The rest of the page stays at normal size. This is particularly useful when you need to read small labels on a diagram or check a footnote reference without losing your place in the main text.

The lens size and zoom level are adjustable in settings."
