---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select a topic area or paste a list of concepts from your course material and click 'Study Path'.

AssisT analyses the concepts and generates a sequential learning path: 'Start with A, because it's foundational. Then B, which builds on A. Then C and D in parallel, because they're independent. Then E, which requires both C and D.'

This prerequisite mapping is something students typically have to figure out through trial and error — reading something that doesn't make sense because they haven't read the prerequisite material first.

The Study Path makes that structure explicit and actionable, which is particularly valuable at the start of a new module or when revising for exams."
