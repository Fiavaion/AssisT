## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the Chrome Web Store — the link is in the video description — and search for AssisT.

Click 'Add to Chrome', then confirm by clicking 'Add extension'.

That's it. AssisT installs in under thirty seconds.

Once installed, the AI Setup wizard opens automatically in a new tab. Let me walk you through it — every step has a Skip button if you want to come back to it later.

Click 'Start scan'. AssisT checks your device for Graphics, Device Memory, Local AI, and On-Device AI — this takes a few seconds.

On the next screen you'll see 'Our Recommendation' — the best AI mode for your device. You can accept it or choose a different one. Click 'Use this mode'.

The 'Configure Your AI' step depends on which mode was recommended:

- For Browser AI, you can download the model now or let it download automatically on first use.
- For Cloud AI, choose your provider — Anthropic, OpenAI, or Google — and paste in your API key. Click 'Verify key' to confirm it works.
- For Local AI, AssisT checks whether Ollama is running and shows you your installed models.

The next screen asks how you prefer AI responses — detail level, reading level, and whether you prefer reading or listening. Pick whatever suits you.

AssisT then runs a quick test — it sends a simple question to your AI and shows the response. When it looks good, click 'Looks good'.

Finally, you'll see a list of Suggested Features based on your preferences. Enable the ones you want, then click 'Apply and finish'.

Click 'Open AssisT' to go to the main popup.

You can revisit any of this by clicking 'Configure AI' inside the AssisT popup at any time."
