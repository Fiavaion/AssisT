# Annotations

**Category: Reading Aids** | Upload order: 15

---

## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Annotations lets you highlight and annotate any text on the web — inline, in a sidebar, and exportable."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select text on any webpage and choose Annotate from the Quick Actions toolbar. Pick a highlight colour and optionally add a note.

The highlight stays on the page every time you return. Your annotation appears in a sidebar panel that collects all your notes for that page in one place.

You can organise annotations by colour — for example, yellow for key concepts, green for things to follow up, pink for questions.

From the sidebar, you can jump back to any annotation on the page, edit your notes, or delete them.

Annotations can be exported as a JSON file and reimported — so you can back them up, share a set of reading notes with a peer, or move them to a different device.

Everything is stored locally in your browser. No data is sent anywhere."
