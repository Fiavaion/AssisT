## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Reduced Motion suppresses animations and transitions across every website you visit — essential for users with vestibular disorders or motion sensitivity."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Toggle Reduced Motion on from the popup.

AssisT injects a CSS rule that sets 'prefers-reduced-motion' behaviour across the page, stopping animations, parallax scrolling effects, auto-playing carousels, and transitions.

This goes beyond the system-level reduced motion setting in Windows and macOS, which not all websites respect. AssisT enforces it directly at the CSS level.

For students with vestibular disorders, even subtle page animations can cause nausea, dizziness, or disorientation. Reduced Motion removes that barrier entirely.

The setting persists across sessions and applies immediately on every page load — no action required on each visit."
