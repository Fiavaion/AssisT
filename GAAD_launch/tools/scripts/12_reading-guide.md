---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Toggle Reading Guide on from the popup or with the keyboard shortcut.

A subtle horizontal line immediately follows your cursor down the page, sitting under the line of text you're reading.

This is modelled on the physical reading rulers many students already use. For students with tracking difficulties, convergence insufficiency, or ADHD, it reduces the cognitive effort of finding your place after a glance away.

You can adjust the colour and thickness of the guide in settings — lighter for less visual impact, darker if you need a stronger anchor.

The guide stays out of the way of clicking and selecting text. It's purely visual — it doesn't change anything about the page."
