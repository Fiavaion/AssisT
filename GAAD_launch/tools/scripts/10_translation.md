## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"To translate an entire page, open the AssisT popup, go to the Look Up Words tab, and toggle Translation on. Select your target language from the dropdown and the whole page translates in place — layout preserved, no new tab.

To translate just a passage, select the text and click Translate in the Quick Actions toolbar. The translation appears in a panel alongside the original.

AssisT supports over fourteen languages including Irish, Polish, Portuguese, Arabic, and Mandarin — reflecting the diverse student population in Irish higher education.

The translated text is fully compatible with Read Aloud: tap the read button and AssisT will read the translation in the appropriate voice for that language, if one is installed on your system."
