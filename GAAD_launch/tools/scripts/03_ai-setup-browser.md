## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the AssisT popup and click on the AI tab, then click Set up AI — that will launch the AI Setup wizard.

If you've just installed the extension, this wizard opens automatically.

Click 'Start Scan'. AssisT checks your device — GPU support, available memory, and whether any other AI options are available. This takes a few seconds.

On the next screen you'll see 'Our Recommendation' — for this device, Browser AI is the best fit. Click 'Use this mode'.

Now you can choose which model to download. Click the dropdown — you'll see a list of models filtered by what your system can handle. On this device we can go up to 5.4 GB.

The bigger the model, generally speaking, the more accurate it will be and the less likely it is to produce incorrect answers. For this demo I'll pick the 650 MB model to keep the download quick.

Click 'Download Model'. The model downloads once and is cached — future uses load instantly.

A quick note on performance: Browser AI is the easiest to set up, but it's the slowest of the three modes. If you want better speed and still want full privacy, the Local AI option using Ollama is worth a look — that's the next video in this series.

Once the download completes, click Continue.

This next screen adjusts how AI features respond. If you prefer shorter answers, click Brief. You can also set a reading level and whether you prefer reading or listening to AI responses. Then click Continue.

AssisT now runs a quick test — it sends a sample question to your AI and shows the response. When it looks good, click 'Looks good'.

Finally you'll see a list of suggested features. Enable the ones that interest you, then click 'Apply and finish'.

That's Browser AI set up and ready to go."
