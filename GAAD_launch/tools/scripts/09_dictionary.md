## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Double-click any word on any webpage, or select it and click the Dictionary icon in the Quick Actions toolbar.

A panel slides in on the right with the definition, pronunciation, part of speech, and example sentences.

You can listen to the pronunciation by clicking the audio icon.

If the word has multiple meanings — as many academic words do — all definitions are listed, with the most common usage first.

You can also read the dictionary entry aloud using Read Aloud, which is particularly useful for complex or technical vocabulary.

The Dictionary works offline after the first lookup — it caches definitions locally so you're not dependent on a connection."
