## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Speed Read uses Rapid Serial Visual Presentation to display text one word at a time — letting you read faster with less eye movement."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select the text you want to speed-read — or leave nothing selected to use the full page — then click Speed Read from the Quick Actions toolbar.

A centred display appears showing one word at a time at the speed you set.

AssisT highlights the pivot letter — the optimal fixation point in each word — in a contrasting colour. This is the technique used by commercial speed-reading apps, built directly into AssisT.

You can set the words-per-minute speed with a slider. Start around two hundred and fifty and increase as you get comfortable.

Use the spacebar to pause and resume. Press Escape to close the reader.

RSVP is particularly useful for students who struggle with tracking across a line but can read individual words well — it removes the need for eye movement entirely."
