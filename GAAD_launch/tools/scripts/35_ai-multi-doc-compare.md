## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Open the Multi-Doc Compare panel from the AssisT popup.

Paste or drag in two or more text passages — excerpts from different journal articles, competing theoretical positions, different versions of a document.

Click Compare. AssisT analyses all the texts together and returns a structured comparison:

Shared themes: what all sources agree on. Unique arguments: what each source contributes that the others don't cover. Contradictions: where the sources directly disagree, and what each one's reasoning is.

This is invaluable for literature reviews, comparative essays, and any assignment that requires you to synthesise multiple sources rather than just summarise each one individually.

You can have the comparison read aloud, copy it as formatted text, or use it as the outline for your writing."
