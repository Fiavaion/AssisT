## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select a passage you've just read and click 'Socratic Tutor' from the Quick Actions toolbar.

AssisT generates a set of probing questions: 'What evidence supports this claim?', 'How does this contradict what you read earlier?', 'What would happen if this assumption were wrong?'

These aren't quiz questions testing recall — they're Socratic questions designed to prompt critical thinking about the material.

This is particularly useful for exam preparation and essay planning: working through the questions forces you to engage with the material at a deeper level than re-reading.

You can answer the questions in a text field and ask AssisT to give feedback on your responses — turning it into a genuine dialogue about the content."
