## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Before opening AssisT, you need to install Ollama. Go to ollama.com — the link is in the description — and download the installer for your system. It's available for Windows, Mac, and Linux.

Run the installer. Once Ollama is installed, open a terminal — that's Command Prompt or PowerShell on Windows, Terminal on Mac.

Type: ollama pull qwen3:8b-q4_K_M

This downloads a high-quality 8 billion parameter model. It's about 5 GB, so it may take a few minutes depending on your connection. You only do this once.

Once the download is complete, Ollama runs automatically in the background — you don't need to keep the terminal open.

Now open the AssisT popup, go to the AI tab, and click Set up AI.

Click 'Start Scan'. AssisT detects that Ollama is running and lists it as an available option. Click 'Use this mode' on Local AI.

The next screen shows your installed models. Select the model you just pulled — qwen3:8b-q4_K_M — and click Continue.

Set your preferences: response length, reading level, and whether you prefer reading or listening. Then click Continue.

AssisT runs a quick test — it sends a sample question to your local model and shows the response. Local AI is noticeably faster than Browser AI. When it looks good, click 'Looks good'.

Enable the suggested features you want and click 'Apply and finish'.

Local AI is now set up and running entirely on your machine."
