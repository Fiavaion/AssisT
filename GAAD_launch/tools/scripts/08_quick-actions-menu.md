## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Select any text on any webpage.

A small floating toolbar appears immediately above your selection. No keyboard shortcut needed, no menu to navigate.

From the toolbar you can: read the selected text aloud, look it up in the Dictionary, translate it, simplify it with AI, or summarise it.

Every action works on exactly the text you selected — nothing more.

The toolbar is designed to be unobtrusive. It disappears the moment you click away or deselect the text.

You can also access the full Quick Actions menu through the keyboard shortcut shown in your AssisT settings, which is useful for keyboard-only users."
