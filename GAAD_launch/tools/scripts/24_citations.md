## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"When you're on any webpage — a journal article, a news source, a research database — click the Citations icon in the AssisT toolbar.

AssisT automatically extracts the page title, URL, author information if available, and access date, and saves it as a citation.

Your saved citations appear in the Citations panel, organised by date added. You can add notes to any citation — 'used in section 2', 'check this source'.

When you're ready to write up your bibliography, click Export. AssisT generates a properly formatted list in APA, MLA, or Harvard style — whichever you select.

This is particularly useful for students who struggle with executive function and lose track of sources mid-assignment. One click at the moment of reading means nothing gets forgotten."
