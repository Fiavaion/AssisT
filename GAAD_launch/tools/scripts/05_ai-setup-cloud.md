---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"First, get your API key. For Gemini, go to aistudio.google.com/apikey — the link is in the description. Sign in with your Google account and click 'Create API key'. Copy it.

For Anthropic Claude, go to console.anthropic.com/settings/keys, create an account, and click 'Create Key'. You'll get free credits to start with.

Now open the AssisT popup, go to the AI tab, and click Set up AI.

Click 'Start Scan'. On the recommendation screen, scroll down and select Cloud AI. Click 'Use this mode'.

Choose your provider — Gemini or Anthropic — then paste in your API key. Click 'Verify key'. AssisT sends a test call to confirm the key works. You'll see a green tick when it's verified.

Click Continue.

Set your preferences: response length, reading level, and learning style. Then click Continue.

AssisT runs a live test with your cloud model. Cloud AI typically responds faster than local options and produces higher quality output for complex tasks like Knowledge Graph and Study Path. When the response looks good, click 'Looks good'.

Enable the suggested features you want and click 'Apply and finish'.

Cloud AI is now active. You can switch providers or update your key at any time from the AI tab in the popup."
