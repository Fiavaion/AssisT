## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Click the AssisT icon in your toolbar to open the popup. If you don't see it, click the puzzle piece icon and pin AssisT.

At the top of the popup you'll see a yellow notification: 'Currently active on LMS sites'. This means AssisT is active on your college's learning platforms by default, but not on the rest of the web yet. Click 'Enable Everywhere' to turn on full accessibility support on any page you visit. You'll need to reload any open tabs after granting that permission.

You may also see a notification about local files. If you want AssisT to work on PDFs and files you open directly in Chrome, grant that permission too — it only appears on some systems.

Now click 'Discover Your Tools'. This is a short quiz that helps AssisT suggest the right features for how you learn. Answer a few questions and AssisT will highlight the features most relevant to you. It takes under a minute.

Once the quiz is done you're back in the main popup. At the very top you'll see the AI mode selector — this shows which AI is currently active. You can switch between Browser AI, Local AI, Cloud AI, or Off at any time from here.

That's your setup done. No account, no login, everything is saved automatically."
