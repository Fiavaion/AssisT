## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Toggle Dyslexia Mode on from the AssisT popup.

The first technique is bionic reading: the first few letters of every word are bolded, giving your eyes an anchor point to jump to. Many dyslexic readers find this significantly reduces re-reading.

The second is syllable colour-coding: each syllable in a word alternates between two colours, making the internal structure of words visible and helping with decoding.

The third is grammar colour-coding: nouns, verbs, and adjectives each get a distinct colour, which helps students separate meaning from the mechanics of parsing a sentence.

You can enable any combination of these three — they don't have to be used together.

All three apply instantly across the entire page, with no page reload."
