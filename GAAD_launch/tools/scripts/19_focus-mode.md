## [INTRO — speak during the 5-second chapter animation]

"Focus Mode dims everything on the page except the area around your cursor — creating a spotlight that guides your attention through the content."

---

## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Toggle Focus Mode on from the popup or with the keyboard shortcut.

The page darkens immediately, with a clear rectangular window following your cursor through the text. Only the content inside the window is fully visible — everything else fades to dark.

The window height is adjustable: narrow for a tight focus on one or two lines, wider for a more comfortable reading zone.

Focus Mode is particularly effective for students with ADHD who find themselves distracted by other content on the page, or for students with visual processing differences who benefit from reducing peripheral noise.

It works alongside Read Aloud — the spotlight will follow the word being read, creating a combined audio-visual focus experience."
