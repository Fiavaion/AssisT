## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"Click into any text field on any webpage — a comment box, a search field, a form, an email — and click the microphone icon that AssisT places near the field.

AssisT activates your microphone and starts transcribing immediately. Speak naturally. Punctuation like 'full stop' and 'comma' is recognised automatically.

The transcribed text appears in real time in the field. You can speak and then edit — the two work seamlessly together.

Speech to Text uses the Web Speech API, which runs entirely on-device in Chrome. Your audio is processed by your browser, not a cloud service.

It works in any text field on any website — search boxes, comment forms, document editors, messaging apps.

To stop, click the microphone icon again or press the keyboard shortcut."
