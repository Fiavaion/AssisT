## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"To activate Read Aloud, click the AssisT icon and toggle it on — or simply select text on any page and click Read Aloud in the Quick Actions toolbar that appears.

AssisT will start reading, highlighting each word as it's spoken.

You can pause, rewind, and skip forward using the floating controls.

In Settings, you can choose from every voice installed on your system, adjust the reading speed from nought-point-five to four times, and control pitch and volume independently.

Read Aloud works on any page — articles, documents, emails, assignment briefs, research papers — anywhere there's text.

Everything happens in your browser. No audio is ever sent to a server."
