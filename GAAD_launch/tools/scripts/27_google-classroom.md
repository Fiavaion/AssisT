## [DEMO — speak while demonstrating on screen]

"On Google Classroom, AssisT activates with the 'All Sites' permission enabled.

In the Stream, AssisT can read announcements and posts aloud. On the Classwork tab, individual assignment descriptions are accessible through Read Aloud and the Quick Actions toolbar.

When you click into a Google Doc or Form linked from Classroom, AssisT's features are active there too — so Read Aloud, Dictionary, and Annotations all work on the full content of the assignment.

This makes AssisT useful across an increasingly common scenario: students using Canvas as the primary LMS but with Google Workspace — Docs, Slides, Classroom — integrated alongside it.

Google Classroom support requires the 'All Sites' permission, which you grant once in the AssisT popup."
