Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is how a plant turns light, water and carbon dioxide into sugar and oxygen.

Light reactions

Chlorophyll in the thylakoid membrane absorbs light and splits water, releasing oxygen.

The Calvin cycle

The stroma reactions fix carbon dioxide into a three carbon sugar without needing light directly.

Rubisco

Rubisco is the enzyme that fixes carbon dioxide, and it is the most abundant protein on Earth.
