## Usage Instructions

1. **Generate base images with AI:**
   - Copy each prompt text into your image generation AI (DALL-E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion)
   - The AI will create backgrounds and text with EMPTY SPACES where noted
   - Use exact dimensions specified (Chrome Web Store requirements)

2. **Composite logo in Photoshop:**
   - Open generated base image in Photoshop
   - Import `Ident/AssisTLogo_1024.jpg` as new layer
   - Resize and position logo in the placeholder space (dimensions provided in each prompt)
   - Ensure logo is centered and properly sized
   - Flatten and export final PNG

3. **Color codes for consistency:**
   - Deep Navy Blue: `#21405f` (PRIMARY BACKGROUND)
   - Cyan: `#6DD5E8`
   - Lime Green: `#A8D88E`
   - Coral: `#FF8C61`
   - White: `#FFFFFF`

4. **Target audience:**
   - General web users who want AI assistance
   - People who read a lot online (articles, docs, emails)
   - Privacy-conscious users who want local AI option
   - Anyone who wants to browse smarter with AI help

5. **Core differentiators to emphasize:**
   - **AI Flexibility:** Local AI (private, fast) OR Cloud AI (powerful)
   - **Universal:** Works on ANY website (news, docs, social, email, etc.)
   - **Privacy-First:** Your data stays yours
   - **Everyday Use:** Not just for studying - for all web browsing

---

## Marketing Message Guidelines

**DO:**

- Emphasize **AI-powered intelligence** (local and cloud options)
- Show **everyday scenarios** (reading news, browsing docs, checking email)
- Highlight **privacy and user choice** (local vs cloud AI)
- Position as a **smart browser companion** (like Arc, Notion AI, ChatGPT)
- Focus on **universal utility** (works everywhere, helps everyone)

**DON'T:**

- Limit to academic/student use cases
- Over-focus on learning disabilities (universal design instead)
- Ignore the AI aspect (it's the key differentiator!)
- Show only one type of content (demonstrate versatility)
- Downplay privacy (local AI option is unique selling point)

**Key Positioning:**
_"AssisT is your AI-powered browser companion that helps you read, understand, and remember anything online - with full control over your privacy and data."_
