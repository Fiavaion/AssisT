## Feature Details

### 1. Smart Summarization

**What it does**: Condenses long texts into brief, detailed, or bullet-point summaries. Includes "ELI5" mode for simplified explanations.

**Who benefits**:
- **ADHD**: Reduces cognitive load, gets to key points faster
- **Cognitive impairment**: Simplifies information processing
- **Time-pressed students**: Quick content overview

---

### 2. Text Simplification

**What it does**: Rewrites complex text in simpler language at three levels (moderate, simple, very simple) while preserving meaning.

**Who benefits**:
- **Dyslexia**: Reduces decoding burden
- **ESL students**: Accessible vocabulary
- **Intellectual disability**: Age-appropriate simplification
- **Anxiety**: Less overwhelming content

---

### 3. Assignment Breakdown

**What it does**: Transforms assignment instructions into step-by-step checklists with time estimates and priority indicators.

**Who benefits**:
- **ADHD**: Breaks overwhelming tasks into manageable steps
- **Executive dysfunction**: Provides structure and sequence
- **Anxiety**: Reduces uncertainty about what's required
- **Autism**: Clear, explicit instructions

---

### 4. Socratic Tutor

**What it does**: Generates thought-provoking questions (comprehension, analysis, synthesis, evaluation) to deepen understanding without giving answers.

**Who benefits**:
- **All learners**: Develops critical thinking
- **Passive learners**: Encourages active engagement
- **Exam preparation**: Self-testing capability

---

### 5. Emotional TTS

**What it does**: Detects emotional tone in text and adjusts speech rate, pitch, and volume for more natural, expressive reading.

**Who benefits**:
- **Autism**: Better understanding of emotional context
- **Auditory processing disorder**: Varied pacing aids comprehension
- **Visual impairment**: Richer audio experience
- **Engagement**: More interesting to listen to

---

### 6. Image Understanding

**What it does**: Generates AI descriptions of images using the LLaVA vision model. Supports brief, detailed, and comprehensive descriptions.

**Who benefits**:
- **Visual impairment/Blindness**: Access to visual content
- **Cognitive impairment**: Explains complex diagrams
- **Learning disabilities**: Alternative format for visual information

---

### 7. Cognitive Profile

**What it does**: Learns user preferences over time to personalize AI responses. Tracks what works best for each individual.

**Who benefits**:
- **All conditions**: Increasingly personalized support
- **Variable needs**: Adapts to changing requirements
- **Privacy-conscious**: All data stored locally

---

### 8. Citation Analyzer

**What it does**: Analyzes sources for credibility, bias, and reliability. Provides credibility scoring (0-100), bias detection, and key claims extraction.

**Who benefits**:
- **Research skills development**: Learn to evaluate sources
- **Critical thinking**: Understand bias and reliability
- **Academic integrity**: Better source selection

---

### 9. Knowledge Graph

**What it does**: Visualizes connections between concepts, annotations, and citations in an interactive node-link diagram.

**Who benefits**:
- **Visual learners**: See relationships spatially
- **ADHD**: Engaging, interactive format
- **Complex topics**: Understand how ideas connect
- **Revision**: Quick overview of learned material

---

### 10. Adaptive RSVP

**What it does**: Rapid Serial Visual Presentation (speed reading) that adapts pace based on word complexity. Slows for difficult words, speeds for simple ones.

**Who benefits**:
- **Dyslexia**: Reduces eye-tracking burden
- **ADHD**: Maintains focus with dynamic pacing
- **Slow readers**: Builds reading speed gradually
- **Visual stress**: Single-word focus reduces strain

---

### 11. Struggle Detection

**What it does**: Monitors user behavior patterns (re-reading, long pauses, erratic scrolling) and proactively offers assistance when struggling is detected.

**Who benefits**:
- **All learners**: Help before frustration builds
- **Pride/anxiety barriers**: Assistance offered, not requested
- **Engagement**: Reduces abandonment of difficult content

---

### 12. Cognitive State Monitor

**What it does**: Tracks focus levels, fatigue indicators, and cognitive load over a study session. Suggests breaks and pacing adjustments.

**Who benefits**:
- **ADHD**: External focus monitoring
- **Fatigue management**: Prevents burnout
- **Study planning**: Optimize study session length
- **Self-awareness**: Learn personal patterns

---

### 13. Multi-Document Compare

**What it does**: Compares 2+ text selections to identify common themes, key differences, contradictions, and unique insights. Generates synthesis.

**Who benefits**:
- **Research tasks**: Compare sources efficiently
- **Critical analysis**: Identify agreements/conflicts
- **Essay writing**: Build balanced arguments
- **Revision**: Compare notes with source material

---

### 14. Study Path Generator

**What it does**: Analyzes content and creates a personalized learning roadmap with topics, prerequisites, and suggested sequence.

**Who benefits**:
- **Executive dysfunction**: Removes planning burden
- **ADHD**: Clear structure and sequence
- **Self-directed learners**: Guidance without instructor
- **Complex subjects**: Logical progression through material

---

## Privacy & Compliance

- All AI processing happens locally via Ollama
- No data transmitted to external servers
- FERPA compliant for educational use
- User data can be cleared at any time
