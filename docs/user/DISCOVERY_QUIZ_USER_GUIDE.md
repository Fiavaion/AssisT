## Introduction

The **Discovery Quiz** is a personalized onboarding experience designed to help you find the accessibility and learning tools that work best for you. Rather than overwhelming you with options, the quiz asks 6 simple questions about your learning preferences and challenges, then recommends the most relevant features.

### Who is this for?

- Students who find reading on screen tiring or difficult
- Learners who think faster than they can type
- Anyone who struggles to maintain focus while working online
- Users who experience visual discomfort from bright screens
- Researchers who need help organizing notes and citations
- Non-native English speakers who benefit from translation tools

### Time Required

**2-5 minutes** — The quiz consists of 6 core questions with optional "Tell me more" sections if you want to provide more detail.

---

## Getting Started

### Launching the Discovery Quiz

1. **Click the AssisT extension icon** in your browser toolbar
2. **Select "Discover Your Tools"** button in the popup header
3. The quiz opens in a **new full-screen tab** for a distraction-free experience

> **Tip:** You can also access the quiz at any time from the extension settings.

### Before You Begin

- No preparation needed — just answer honestly based on your experiences
- There are no right or wrong answers
- You can go back and change answers at any time
- Your responses are stored locally and never shared

### Feature Cards Explained

Each recommended feature includes:

1. **Icon** — Visual identifier for the feature category
2. **Name** — The feature's name
3. **Brief Description** — One-line summary of what it does

## Applying Your Personalized Profile

### Review Your Selections

Before applying, you can:

- **Toggle features ON/OFF** — Disable any recommendations you don't want
- **Learn more** — Click to expand details about each feature
- **Retake quiz** — Start over if your needs have changed

### Apply Your Settings

1. Review the recommended features
2. Toggle off any you don't want enabled immediately
3. Click **"Apply & Close"**

Your settings are saved immediately and the quiz tab closes.

### Try Before You Commit

Click **"Try Demo Page"** to:

- Open a sample page with realistic educational content
- Test your recommended features in action
- See how tools like TTS, highlighting, and overlays work together
- Make informed decisions before enabling features site-wide

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I retake the quiz?

**Yes.** Access the Discovery Quiz at any time from the extension popup. Your previous responses are not saved — each session starts fresh.

### Will this change my current settings?

**Only if you click "Apply & Close."** Browsing through the quiz doesn't modify your settings. You have full control over what gets enabled.

### What if I don't see a feature I want?

The quiz recommends features based on your responses. All features are available in the full settings panel regardless of quiz results. Access **Settings → All Features** to see everything.

### Can I change my profile later?

**Absolutely.** Enable or disable any feature at any time from the extension popup. The quiz is just a starting point — you're always in control.

### Is my data shared?

**No.** All responses and settings are stored locally on your device. Nothing is sent to external servers.

### What if I accidentally close the tab?

No problem — just reopen the quiz from the extension popup. Since responses aren't saved until you click Apply, you'll start fresh.

## Accessibility Features

The Discovery Quiz was built with accessibility as a priority:

### WCAG 2.2 AA Compliance

- **Keyboard Navigation** — Full functionality without a mouse
- **Screen Reader Support** — Proper ARIA labels and live regions
- **Focus Indicators** — Clear visible focus states on all elements
- **Text Spacing** — Compliant with SC 1.4.12
- **Touch Targets** — Minimum 44x44px for all interactive elements

### Visual Accessibility

- **Colour Contrast** — 4.5:1 minimum for all text
- **Reduced Motion** — Respects `prefers-reduced-motion` setting
- **No Time Limits** — Complete at your own pace
- **Clear Typography** — Legible fonts and adequate sizing

### Cognitive Accessibility

- **Progress Indicator** — Always know where you are in the quiz
- **Simple Language** — Questions written in plain English
- **No Pressure** — Skip option available, no scoring judgments
- **Undo Available** — Go back and change answers freely
