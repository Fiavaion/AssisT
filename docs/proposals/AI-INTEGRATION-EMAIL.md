Hi,

I wanted to share an idea we've been exploring that could meaningfully help our students with severe learning difficulties.

## The Concept

We're looking at integrating AI language processing into our AssisT browser extension. The key insight is that modern AI can now run _locally on student devices_—meaning we can provide intelligent support while keeping student data completely private.

## What It Could Do

**Content Simplification** — The highest-impact feature. When a student clicks "Simplify" on a Canvas assignment, AI rewrites complex academic text at an appropriate reading level:

> _Original:_ "Analyse the socioeconomic factors that precipitated the industrial revolution..."
>
> _Simplified:_ "Explain why the Industrial Revolution happened. Focus on: how farming changed, why people moved to cities. _(Socioeconomic = related to money and society)_"

**Writing Assistance** — Building on our existing voice input, AI could correct transcription errors and improve grammar in real-time.

**Visual Content Description** — Going beyond basic OCR to explain what diagrams and charts actually _mean_.

**Contextual Help** — An on-demand assistant that explains concepts without giving away answers.

## Who This Helps

Students with dyslexia, ADHD, autism spectrum conditions, and processing disorders—essentially those who struggle most with complex digital content.

## Privacy-First Approach

This is the key differentiator:

1. **Default:** AI runs entirely in the browser—nothing leaves the device
2. **Optional:** Local server for higher quality (still completely private)
3. **Fallback:** Cloud API only with explicit consent (data anonymised)

We're designing for GDPR compliance from the start, with EU AI Act requirements in mind.
