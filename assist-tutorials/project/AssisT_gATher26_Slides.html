The problem 03

Students face barriers every day on the very platforms we use to teach them.

  1. 01Cost & gatekeeping. TTS tools are expensive, locked to lab machines, or require a formal diagnosis to access.
  2. 02Empty platforms. Canvas, Moodle and Google Classroom ship with no built-in reading or writing support.
  3. 03The discovery gap. First-generation and EAL students rarely know what support exists.
  4. 04The support cliff. Institutional licences end at graduation. The tool a student built four years of habits around — gone, exactly when they need it most.
03 / 14
The product 04

What AssisT is

A Chrome extension that runs quietly alongside every website a student visits.

  • Installs in under a minute from the Chrome Web Store
  • Works on Canvas, Moodle, Google Classroom — and every other site
  • No account required  ·  no data leaves the browser
  • GDPR compliant  ·  FERPA-aligned
  • Free and open source — EUPL-1.2

Built for

  • Dyslexia
  • ADHD
  • Visual impairment
  • Processing differences
  • EAL learners
  • Anxiety

"Because support shouldn't end at graduation."

Reading help

Three features that change how students read online.

01 · Read aloud

Text-to-speech with word highlighting.

Word-by-word highlighting follows the student across any webpage.

02 · Reading mode

Strip the noise. Keep the article.

Removes navigation, ads, sidebars. Renders in the student's preferred font and spacing.

Bionic reading, by default.

Syllable colour-coding and grammar highlighting. Switchable with one click.

04 · OCR

Read what isn't text yet.

Extracts and reads from images, scanned PDFs, screenshots.

Features · 3 of 6 07

Visual comfort

Reducing visual stress — without a clinical referral.

Overlay

Screen colour overlay

Tinted overlay across the whole screen. Colour, intensity, blend mode — fully adjustable. Evidence-based for Meares-Irlen-type visual stress.

Attention

Focus mode

Dark overlay with a clear reading window that follows the cursor. Filters distraction for ADHD and attention difficulties.

Tracking

Reading guide

A horizontal guide line that tracks mouse movement. Supports visual tracking difficulties.

Vestibular

Reduced motion

Suppresses animations globally. Essential for vestibular disorders and sensory sensitivities.

What's next 13

The roadmap ahead

What's coming next.

Reach

Browser support — Firefox & Safari

So AssisT reaches students on every platform, not just Chromium.

Speech

STT voice profile creator

A dedicated voice training tool for users with Dysarthria — calibrating the recognition engine to a student's individual speech patterns, rather than expecting students to adapt to the engine.

Exams

Exam Mode

A password-protected lockdown profile, configured by the AT technician. Only the supports approved on a student's accommodation plan stay active — everything else (AI, web access, summarisation) is locked. No more all-or-nothing workarounds at exam time.

Polish

UI overhaul

A full redesign of the control panel — clearer layout, better keyboard navigation, simpler first-run experience for students new to AT.

Community

Community-led refinement

Shaped by AT technicians and learning support professionals. The goal is not a finished product — it's a tool that keeps improving with the community that uses it.
