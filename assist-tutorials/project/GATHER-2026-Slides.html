About 02

Who I Am

Name Mark Jones Role Digital Technical Officer, Developer Where National College of Art & Design, Dublin Tenure 18+ years at NCAD · 2 years on AT

Students with disabilities navigate a fragmented landscape: expensive proprietary tools, platform-locked licences, and support that varies wildly by institution, country, and diagnosis.

Some students receive excellent support in one college and none in another. Some lose access to tools entirely when they change course or transfer. Others simply never know what's available. AssisT began as a direct response to that fragmentation — a single, consistent tool that goes wherever the student goes.

The Problem 03

Students face barriers every day on the very platforms we use to teach them.

  • 01Cost & gatekeeping. TTS tools are expensive, locked to lab machines, or require a formal diagnosis to access.
  • 02Empty platforms. Canvas, Moodle and Google Classroom ship with no built-in reading or writing support.
  • 03The discovery gap. First-generation and EAL students rarely know what support exists.
  • 04The support cliff. Institutional licences end at graduation. The tool a student built four years of habits around — gone, exactly when they need it most.
The Product 04

What AssisT Is

A Chrome extension that runs quietly alongside every website a student visits.

  • Installs in under a minute from the Chrome Web Store
  • Works on Canvas, Moodle, Google Classroom — and every other site
  • No account required · no data leaves the browser · GDPR & FERPA compliant
  • Free and open source — EUPL-1.2

"Because support shouldn't end at graduation."

Features · 3 of 6 07

Visual Comfort

Overlay

Screen Colour Overlay

Tinted overlay across the whole screen. Colour, intensity, blend mode — fully adjustable. Evidence-based for Meares-Irlen-type visual stress.

Attention

Focus Mode

Dark overlay with a clear reading window that follows the cursor. Filters distraction for ADHD and attention difficulties.

Tracking

Reading Guide

A horizontal guide line that tracks mouse movement. Supports visual tracking difficulties.

Vestibular

Reduced Motion

Suppresses animations globally. Essential for vestibular disorders and sensory sensitivities.

What's Next 13

The Roadmap Ahead

Reach

Browser Support — Firefox & Safari

So AssisT reaches students on every platform, not just Chromium.

Speech

STT Voice Profile Creator

A dedicated voice training tool for users with Dysarthria — calibrating the recognition engine to a student's individual speech patterns, rather than expecting students to adapt to the engine.

Exams

Exam Mode

A password-protected lockdown mode configured by the AT technician — restricting AssisT to only the features approved for a student's exam accommodations. The student retains their permitted supports; everything else is locked until the AT re-enters the password. No more all-or-nothing workarounds at exam time.

Polish & Community

UI Overhaul & Community-led Refinement

A full redesign of the control panel shaped by AT technicians and learning support professionals. The goal is not a finished product — it's a tool that keeps improving with the community that uses it.
