Text for AI Summarization

Neuroplasticity, also known as brain plasticity or neural plasticity, is the ability of neural networks in the brain to change through growth and reorganization. These changes range from individual neuron pathways making new connections to systematic adjustments like cortical remapping. During development, the immature brain is especially plastic and is significantly influenced by experience and environmental factors. Neuroplasticity continues throughout life, albeit at reduced levels compared to childhood, allowing the brain to compensate for injury and adjust to new situations or changes in the environment.

The concept of neuroplasticity has revolutionized neuroscience and medicine by demonstrating that the brain is not a static organ but a dynamic, ever-changing system. This understanding has profound implications for rehabilitation after brain injury, treatment of neurological conditions, and optimization of learning strategies across all ages.
